This recheck covers all 10 grouped trigger entries, all 19 named muscle components, the 8 symptom-site explanations, and the palpation/self-care handbook. It compares authored content with the source portions listed below. It does not claim individual diagnosis, treatment effectiveness for an app protocol, or clinical validation. There is no independent-reviewer approval gate. `reviewedBy: null` remains a factual field; no reviewer or approval was invented.

The 2026-10-19/20 comparisons remain historical records. Sources actually rechecked here have latest `checkedAt: 2026-10-22`. ## Handbook and presentation corrections

- Delphi's three proposed features remain expert consensus, not a diagnostic-accuracy study or a map validation. Referred symptoms need not occupy fixed locations. Local twitch response is not in its essential three-feature cluster.
- The energy-crisis model remains a hypothesis. The nine-volunteer biochemical study compares groups and an uninvolved remote gastrocnemius site; it does not validate calf trigger attribution or a tissue lesion detected by touch.
- Palpation reliability/validity limits remain tied to Myburgh and Nolet. Roller findings concern short-term ROM/recovery outcomes; neither an optimal common dose nor a mechanism of removing fascia restrictions is established by those checked portions.
- Deep-pressure limits now describe the app's conservative choice and documented adverse-event evidence, without saying a missing independent reviewer is the reason.
- Renamed misleading panel labels (“mốc xương”, “cảm giác dưới ngón”) to describe a region and contact limits. The per-entry mobility/loading examples now explicitly say they are app-authored examples, not a studied four-step sequence.
- Removed all “chờ chuyên gia duyệt” product-gate language from owned runtime files and sample prompts. Source-anatomy crops and their original labels remain the main muscle display. Legacy point coordinates/referral paths remain withheld because no source-to-location mapping exists. The symptom locator retains keyboard navigation and coarse navigation-only wording.
- Corrected the unused Travell compatibility reference: it previously linked to Shah's review while naming a book. It now points to the publisher's 3rd-edition product record and states that only that product record was checked. Neither Travell nor Janda was read in full or used to validate maps/protocols.
