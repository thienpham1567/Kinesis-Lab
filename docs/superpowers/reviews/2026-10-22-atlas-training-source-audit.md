## Limits and validation handoff

The literature search is targeted to the finite built-in claims, not an exhaustive systematic review. Much exercise evidence is abstract-only, small-sample and short-duration. “No detected difference” is not proof that all implementations are equivalent. New citations do not establish effects in injured users, all ages, or a particular trainee. Educational exercise cues remain authored options, with no claim of NSCA-manual equivalence.
