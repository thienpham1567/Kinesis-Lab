# Eight-region anatomy education: evidence comparison

Date checked: 2026-10-19. Qualified clinician/anatomist reviewer: **null**. Spatial mapping reviewer: **null**. This is a literature comparison and conservative text correction, not clinical validation.

Scope: every record in `src/lib/clinical/anatomy-atlas.ts`: cervical, shoulder, thoracic/core, lumbar, hip/pelvis, thigh/hamstring, knee and ankle/foot. The region IDs, data interface and exported lookup functions remain available. The per-region source registry and field-level claim mappings are in `src/lib/clinical/evidence-anatomy.ts`. Source numbers are local to each region and one-based; structure indices are zero-based. The registry states what was checked and the access scope, rather than certifying all previous claims.

The complete eight-region dataset was read. All 8 summaries, 8 biomechanics descriptions, 24 structure descriptions, 16 observation entries, 16 activity-adjustment pairs, clinical-context lists, warning lists and example prompts were substantively reviewed and rewritten. There are 56 regional source links representing 52 distinct URLs and 42 field-level claim mappings. Seven records have `literature-reviewed` status; thoracic/core remains `limited` because its prior pressure, posture and postpartum treatment claims exceeded the reviewed evidence. All eight retain `reviewedBy: null`.

## Sources actually inspected

All source titles, URLs, access notes and the 2026-10-19 check date are stored with the corresponding claim mappings in the registry. Anatomical entries were checked against Loyola University Chicago's LUMEN individual muscle pages, not merely its master index. Each cited entry exposes Origin, Action and Insertion. These are older university teaching entries and sometimes use broad/historical terminology; the app uses concise gross descriptions rather than importing exact fascicle maps or declaring the entries authoritative for treatment. OpenStax sections 7.3 and 11.6 were used for vertebral/disc and lower-limb group anatomy. Table prose with obvious inconsistencies was not blindly copied. No textbook image, muscle-page image or protected source passage was copied into the repository.

Key clinical comparisons:

- [Neck Pain: Revision 2017, official APTA Orthopedics PDF](https://www.orthopt.org/uploads/content_files/files/Neck%20Pain%20CPG%20-%20Revision%202017.pdf): full PDF retrieved directly and extracted, with summary A2–A3, differential diagnosis and examination sections inspected. Classification/clinical presentation guide exercise selection; serious pathology needs referral; isolated physical-test findings require caution. This does not support the app's former posture-to-torque rule.
- [Braman et al., shoulder rhythm primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2841046/): full author manuscript, bone-fixed sensor methods, Table I and rhythm interpretation inspected. Rhythm varied with movement phase/arc; this does not make a fixed 2:1 ratio a diagnostic requirement. The study concerns asymptomatic participants and does not establish a pain mechanism or optimal exercise.
- [AAOS/ASES shoulder conditioning](https://www.orthoinfo.org/recovery/rotator-cuff-and-shoulder-conditioning-program): program framing and row/external-rotation examples inspected; used as general examples requiring appropriate exercise selection, not an off-the-shelf prescription for all shoulder pain.
- [EHS rectus diastasis guideline executive summary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10364860/): full HTML, especially KQ2 and KQ4–KQ6. Symptom severity cannot be reliably inferred from width alone, and evidence does not select one exercise regimen. No postpartum rehabilitation plan is supplied.
- [American Heart Association activity and hypertension education](https://www.heart.org/en/health-topics/high-blood-pressure/changes-you-can-make-to-manage-high-blood-pressure/getting-active-to-control-high-blood-pressure): breathing and gradual activity sections inspected. Holding breath can raise pressure; a breathing cue does not guarantee a normal pressure response or replace individualized advice.
- [NICE NG59 recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng59/chapter/Recommendations): official indexed recommendation text 1.1.1, 1.1.4–1.1.6 and 1.2.1–1.2.2 inspected. Maintaining activity and choosing exercise to suit needs/capabilities are supported. No fixed deadlift ban, neutral-spine force reduction or surgical rehabilitation schedule is inferred.
- [Warwick FAI agreement](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/50/19/1169/): publisher indexed diagnosis, symptoms and treatment sections inspected. The triad of symptoms, signs and imaging is required; rehabilitation and activity modification can be options. Morphology alone or a squat observation is insufficient.
- [Hamstring Strain Injury in Athletes 2022, official PDF](https://www.orthopt.org/uploads/content_files/files/Hamstring_Strain_Injury_in_Athletes.pdf): full PDF retrieved/extracted, especially summary CPG2. Eccentric exercise, strength/length evaluation, progressive running and functional return-to-play assessment were checked. Guidance for strain does not establish a universal plan for chronic proximal tendon pain or avulsion.
- [Patellofemoral pain best practice guide 2024](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/58/24/1486): publisher indexed application/discussion/conclusion inspected. Education with knee-targeted exercise, additional hip exercise where indicated, and adjustments for symptom severity/irritability are supported.
- [Aspetar ACL rehabilitation guideline 2023](https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/9/500): publisher indexed full-text exercise-initiation and open/closed chain recommendation passages inspected. Both exercise types have a role; the app no longer supplies one generic safe angle or time schedule for every post-operative person.
- [Midportion Achilles tendinopathy 2024, official PDF](https://www.orthopt.org/uploads/content_files/files/Achilles_Pain_revision_2024.pdf): full PDF retrieved/extracted, summary CPG2, scope and loading guidance inspected. Tendon loading and education are supported within tolerance; complete rest is not a general recommendation. This guideline is explicitly not a rupture or insertional-tendinopathy protocol.
- AAOS OrthoInfo and NHS symptom/referral pages were inspected for [neck neurological warning symptoms](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/neck-sprain/), [shoulder urgent assessment](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/shoulder-pain/), [chest pain](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/chest-pain/), [cauda equina symptoms](https://www.nbt.nhs.uk/our-services/a-z-services/neurosurgery/neurosurgery-patient-information/same-day-emergency-clinic-sdec-cauda-equina-syndrome-sec), [hip fracture](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/broken-hip/), [knee red flags](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/knee-pain/), [thigh injury/avulsion](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/muscle-strains-in-the-thigh), [DVT](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/deep-vein-thrombosis-dvt/), [Achilles rupture](https://www.orthoinfo.org/diseases--conditions/achilles-tendon-rupture-tear) and [bone stress injury](https://orthoinfo.aaos.org/en/diseases--conditions/stress-fractures/). The Vietnamese text distinguishes urgent assessment from immediate emergency help and does not copy UK service numbers.

Some direct web-reader opens returned 403/404 or a browser challenge even when publisher-indexed full-text passages were available. The registry identifies indexed full-text access rather than claiming a full PDF review for those sources. The three official CPG PDFs listed above were retrieved successfully using direct HTTPS and text extraction. Downloaded PDFs/text were temporary research materials, not redistributed app assets.

## Software behavior and verification

Keyword overlap between a client issue and medical condition previously changed a region alert from caution to crimson. That count is not an evidence-based urgency rule, so keyword matches now produce caution navigation cues. Red-flag triage requires clinical information beyond substring overlap.

The targeted check is `node --experimental-strip-types --disable-warning=MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON scripts/check-anatomy-regions-evidence.mjs`. It checks all eight IDs, source/check-date/access metadata, numbered source references, explicit review limitations, pending reviewer state, data shapes, removed misleading legacy claims, and the non-escalation of keyword overlap. It passed for 8/8 regions and 24 structure summaries. Targeted ESLint and `git diff --check` also passed. These checks establish data/software consistency only; they do not prove medical truth, safe prescribing or spatial accuracy.

Outstanding boundaries: no named expert has reviewed the Vietnamese text or 3D mapping; mesh-derived fascicle paths, attachment footprints and measurements remain unvalidated. This work is not a complete audit of every app dataset or a systematic review of every condition mentioned. Older source publication dates and narrower populations are explicit. Individualized exercise dosing, postoperative protocols and return-to-sport clearance remain outside these region cards.
