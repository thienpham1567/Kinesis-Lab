## App decisions

The interface can present the same sourced identity more economically by separating languages and putting an explicitly named subdivision after its parent. Example English display: Pectoralis major · sternocostal head; original catalog: Sternocostal head of pectoralis major muscle. Removing a generic terminal muscle in a muscle context does not license dropping a differentiating head, part, belly, number or hand/foot qualifier. Source values, IDs and evidence comparisons remain unchanged. Existing source-only and limited/unresolved translation statuses remain unchanged; no professional approval is introduced.

This source comparison supports the presentation convention and named examples. Automated catalog checks establish preservation/distinguishability, not independent medical validation of all 1,031 source structures.
