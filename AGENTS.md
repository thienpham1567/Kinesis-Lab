## Medical accuracy

The user requires medically accurate knowledge grounded in reputable sources. Follow `docs/medical-evidence-policy.md` for new anatomical/medical content. Keep claim-level citations and access/review dates. Source comparison, code tests and visual realism do not establish clinical validation. Do not present mesh-derived or AI-generated fiber directions, landmarks or tissue measurements as verified anatomy. Preserve explicit pending expert review status until a qualified reviewer actually approves the content and spatial mapping.
